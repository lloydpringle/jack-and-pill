Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version: everything you enter in Jack & Pill stays on your own device. There's no account, no tracking, no ads, and we never see or sell your data.

Who we are

Jack & Pill is a supplement and habit tracker made by Lloyd Pringle, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Lloyd Pringle, and "the app" means Jack & Pill on the web and on iPhone.

What the app stores

To work, the app keeps the information you choose to enter:

  • the supplements in your stack, with doses, schedules and stock levels if you track them
  • your daily logs: what you took, skipped, and any skip reason you picked
  • your weekly check-ins, including how you felt and any note you added
  • your settings, such as reminder time, weekly target and how Jack looks
  • simple counts of which features you've used, shown to you under "Tester stats" in Settings

All of this is saved on your device only. On the web it's kept in your browser's storage; in the iPhone app it's kept on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't collect

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or any account details.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts or photos.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data, because we never receive it.

Hosting

The web version is hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel may process basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the page to you. This is handled under Vercel's privacy policy. We don't use it to identify you.

Reminders

If the iPhone app offers reminders and you allow notifications, they are scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Payments

If an optional Pro upgrade is offered, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Sharing

The app can create a summary card of your week. It's only shared if you choose to share it, and only with the people or apps you pick.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you're in control of it. You can erase everything at any time with "Reset all data" in the app's Settings. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's data for the web version, also removes it.

Not medical advice

Jack & Pill helps you keep track of habits. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always talk to a qualified professional about your health and what you take.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. In any case, the app stores nothing on our side.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
